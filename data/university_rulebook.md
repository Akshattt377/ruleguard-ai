# University Academic and Student Regulations

## 1. Purpose and Scope
These regulations establish a common framework for academic eligibility, student responsibilities, examinations, fees, scholarships, residential life, discipline, and grievances. They apply to all enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students unless a programme-specific rule is formally approved and published. Students are responsible for reading current notices, maintaining records, and submitting requests through the designated office. An oral assurance is not a substitute for a written order.

## 2. Attendance Requirements
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated from scheduled instructional sessions, excluding officially cancelled classes. A student below the threshold must normally submit an explanation to the department before the examination form is approved. The department may require additional academic work, but such work does not automatically replace the attendance requirement.

## 3. Medical Exemption
A student whose attendance is affected by a documented medical condition may apply for an attendance concession. The application must include a certificate from a registered medical practitioner, dates of incapacity, and verification by the medical board. The ordinary concession may reduce the required attendance to 65% where the board certifies that the absence was medically necessary. Approval is not automatic and must be recorded in writing.

## 4. Examination Eligibility and Contradiction Notice
The examination cell shall normally reject forms from students below 75% attendance. However, an approved medical concession under Section 3 may permit eligibility at 65%. Where the two provisions appear to apply, the examination cell must place the case before the Academic Eligibility Committee rather than deciding informally.

## 5. Academic Calendar
The academic year contains two teaching semesters and a supplementary examination period. The Registrar publishes the calendar before registration. Changes caused by public emergencies, accreditation requirements, or government directions must be announced through the official student portal.

## 6. Examination Conduct
Students must carry valid identity documents and arrive before the reporting time. Possession of unauthorized notes, communication devices, or assistance materials may be treated as academic misconduct. The invigilator records incidents and forwards them to the disciplinary authority. Students must not obstruct an investigation or contact witnesses to influence their statements.

## 7. Revaluation
A student may request revaluation of eligible written papers within seven calendar days of result publication. The application must identify the course, examination session, and fee receipt. Revaluation is not available for practical, project, attendance, or viva components unless a programme rule expressly provides otherwise. The revised result may increase, decrease, or remain unchanged.

## 8. Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail a limited number of courses may apply for a supplementary examination during the notified window. The examination cell publishes the eligible course list, fee, and timetable. A student with an unresolved disciplinary case may be prevented from registering until the case is decided.

## 9. Scholarships
Scholarship applications require the prescribed form, income documents, academic record, and bank details. Students must report material changes in family income or enrolment status. A scholarship may be suspended for false information, prolonged non-attendance, or failure to meet the funding agency's conditions. The university cannot guarantee payment dates controlled by an external agency.

## 10. Fee Deadlines
| Fee category | Normal deadline | Late window |
|---|---|---|
| Semester tuition | 15 July | 16–22 July |
| Examination fee | 10 November | 11–15 November |
| Hostel fee | 20 July | 21–27 July |
| Library fine settlement | Before registration | No automatic late window |

Fees paid during a late window attract the notified late charge. A student facing financial hardship may submit a written request for installment consideration before the deadline. Submission of a request does not itself extend the deadline.

## 11. Hostel Rules
Residents must follow room-allocation, visitor, quiet-hour, safety, and maintenance instructions. Visitors must be registered at the designated desk. Cooking with prohibited appliances, damaging common property, or keeping unauthorized occupants may lead to disciplinary action. A resident may appeal a hostel penalty through the student grievance process.

## 12. Discipline
Disciplinary proceedings should provide notice of the allegation and a reasonable opportunity to respond. The authority may impose warning, fine, restriction, suspension, or expulsion according to seriousness and applicable law. Interim measures may be used to protect safety or preserve evidence. A written order should state the findings and available review route.

## 13. Committee Powers
The Academic Eligibility Committee may review attendance concessions, examination eligibility exceptions, and related academic cases. Its decision must be recorded and communicated by the examination cell. The committee may request documents and may return an incomplete application for clarification. It cannot waive a statutory requirement unless a competent authority has delegated that power in writing.

## 14. Student Grievances
A student should first submit a grievance to the concerned department within fifteen working days of the event. If unresolved, the student may appeal to the Student Grievance Committee within ten working days after the departmental response. The committee issues a reasoned decision. A grievance does not automatically suspend an academic deadline.

## 15. Review of Grievance Decisions
A review request may be submitted to the Dean of Students within seven working days of the Student Grievance Committee decision where there is new evidence, procedural unfairness, or a material error. The review authority may confirm, modify, or remand the decision. This review route is separate from any statutory remedy.

## 16. Leave Policy
Leave should be requested in advance through the portal except in emergencies. Medical leave requires supporting documentation. Personal travel, family functions, internships, and private commitments are not automatically treated as authorized academic absence. Approval of leave does not guarantee an attendance concession unless the applicable attendance provision is separately satisfied.

## 17. Student Records and Privacy
Students must keep contact information current. Academic records are released only to the student or an authorized recipient, subject to applicable law. Requests for correction should identify the record, explain the error, and attach supporting evidence. The university may retain records for audit and accreditation purposes.

## 18. Safety and Emergency Management
Students must follow evacuation, laboratory, fire, and emergency instructions. Safety incidents should be reported immediately. The university may temporarily close facilities or alter schedules to protect the community. Official emergency notices override informal messages but should be archived for later reference.

## 19. Interpretation
Where two provisions appear inconsistent, the more specific provision should be reviewed alongside the later official notification and any delegated authority. Students should not rely on social-media summaries. The responsible office must issue a written clarification where the conflict affects eligibility, payment, discipline, or a deadline.

## 20. Publication and Amendments
These regulations are published on the official portal. Amendments take effect on the date stated in the notification and are not effective merely because they are discussed in a meeting. Departments must maintain a version history and archive superseded copies. Students should consult the current version before acting.
