# Planted Contradictions

1. **Attendance vs Medical Exemption**
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Attendance Requirements**: requires 75% attendance.
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Medical Exemption**: approved medical concession may reduce the requirement to 65%.
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Examination Eligibility and Contradiction Notice**: sends overlapping cases to the Academic Eligibility Committee.

2. **Fee deadline vs hardship request**
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Fee Deadlines**: a hardship request does not itself extend the deadline.
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Fee Deadlines**: late-window payment attracts a late charge.
   - This is a conditional tension, not a universal cancellation of the deadline.

3. **Grievance route vs review route**
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Student Grievances**: department → Student Grievance Committee.
   - `university_rulebook.md`, **Review of Grievance Decisions**: Dean of Students review after committee decision.
   - These are sequential routes and must not be collapsed into one step.
